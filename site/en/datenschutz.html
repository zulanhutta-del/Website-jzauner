2. In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services and loads nothing from third-party servers. Fonts and images are delivered by the host of this website. I only process personal data when you contact me.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA, and delivered from a data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. When you visit, the server automatically stores technical data (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system). This data is used only to keep the site secure and stable (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and is deleted according to Vercel's rules, usually after a few days.

As Vercel is based in the USA, data may be transferred to the USA. This is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Vercel is certified under it, Art. 45 GDPR) or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). A data processing agreement (Vercel Data Processing Addendum) is in place via the terms of service (Art. 28 GDPR).

4. Contact form

The contact form on this website does not store or send anything itself. It assembles a message from your input and opens it in WhatsApp or your email app. I only receive your data (name, topic, optional date, message and how you heard about me) once you send it there yourself. I use it only to reply to your message (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete it when it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

5. Contact via WhatsApp, email or Instagram

If you contact me via WhatsApp, WhatsApp Ireland Limited (Meta) processes your message and metadata under its own terms; data may be transferred to the USA. If you prefer not to, please write to me by email. Messages via Instagram are subject to Meta's privacy policy. Links to WhatsApp and Instagram only transfer data once you click them.

6. Cookies and local storage

This website uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, so there is no cookie banner.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to the email address above.

8. Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, www.dsb.gv.at.
