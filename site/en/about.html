About me

I'm Julian Zauner, a photographer and designer from Thalgau near Salzburg, Austria. I trained in graphic and media design at HTL Salzburg and discovered photography and video there. It hasn't let go of me since.

Today I combine three things: photography, design and AI. You get the pictures and the design around them. And with AI I build myself tools that make my everyday work easier.

I'm looking forward to putting my passion for photography and design into exciting projects, and I keep learning along the way.
