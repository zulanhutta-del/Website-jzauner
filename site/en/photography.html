Photography

I'm Julian Zauner, a photographer based near Salzburg, Austria. I photograph portraits, events and graduation balls, concerts, sport, landscapes, wildlife – and above all cars and motorsport. In Salzburg, across Austria and on the road.

Gallery

Photography from Salzburg

What I shoot: portraits in the studio and outdoors, graduation balls, parties and concerts, ice hockey, freestyle motocross and skiing, mountains, coasts and wildlife – and above all car shoots with rolling shots and details, plus race days on track.

How I work: right in the action rather than on the sidelines, with an eye for light and speed, and I edit every photo myself.

Where: based in Thalgau near Salzburg. I shoot in Salzburg, across Austria and on the road, for example in Monaco, Cannes and Corsica.
